Patterns · Engineering playbook

Named, transferable approaches I reach for across projects.

01 · For multi-platform ERP integration

Canonical Model

One normalized schema. Map at the boundary. Validate before write. Reject inputs that violate invariants; surface the why.
02 · For high-performance Angular data UIs

Syncfusion Data-Grid

Virtualized rendering, server-side paging, row-model abstraction. The pattern that made 10K-row tables usable in production.
03 · For extending third-party commerce platforms

nopCommerce Plugin

Compose features without forking. Hook points over rewrites. Surviving platform upgrades through disciplined extension surfaces.
04 · For delaying releases with receipts

Ship-Faster Pushback

When QA wants a date and the system isn't ready: name the risk, document the call, defer with receipts. "We cannot ship a weak and faulty product."
