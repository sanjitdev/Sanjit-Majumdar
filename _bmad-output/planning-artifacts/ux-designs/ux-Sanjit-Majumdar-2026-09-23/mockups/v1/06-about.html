01 · Who I am

The short version.

I'm Sanjit. I work in Dhaka, Bangladesh — UTC+6 — and I've been writing software professionally since November 2018. I lead a small team at Brain Station 23 PLC, an enterprise software house. The work is mostly .NET, Angular, TypeScript, and SQL Server, plus whatever the integration story requires.

I care about three things in roughly this order: does it work, can someone else understand it, and does it stay working. The first one is the bar for shipping. The second is the bar for being on a team. The third is what separates code from software.

I don't write marketing copy about myself well, but I do write code that other engineers can read six months later. If that's the version of me you need, the case studies on the Work page are the receipt.
