02 / Philosophy

How I think about the work.

Three beliefs I keep returning to. Not principles I learned in a book — patterns I noticed across seven years of shipping.

Right-size architecture to the team that has to operate it.

Microservices look great on a whiteboard. They're a different story at 2am when the on-call rotation is two people. The right architecture is the one your team can actually run, not the one that scales to 100x traffic you'll never have.

Ship the smallest honest thing first.

A read-only reconciliation view beats a write-back platform that's been "two weeks from shipping" for six months. Ship the smallest version that does what it says, then grow it. Users trust what they can verify against their Excel.

Write down what you learned, while you still remember the cost.

The best engineering playbook entries are written three weeks after the project shipped, not three years later. The hard parts are still fresh, the rationalizations haven't settled in yet. Capture the playbook while the scar tissue is still tender.
